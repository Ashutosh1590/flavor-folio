Plating Guide
Duck Confit · Sauté Station
Plate Reference Photo
📸
No photo added yet

Plating Sequence
1
Warm plate in pass (60°C) · 30 sec
2
Swoosh jus across plate from 7 → 1 o'clock
3
Quenelle celeriac purée at 9 o'clock · 60g
4
Place duck leg, skin-side up, centre plate
5
Stack haricots verts against purée
6
Drop 3 jus dots · scatter 5 micro herbs
7
Wipe rim · call the pass
Chef's Notes
Wipe the rim before service. Duck must rest 2 min off heat before plating. Purée should hold the haricots, not pool. Jus swoosh goes down first, always.
